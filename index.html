Day 1 · Treviso → Pisa Easy

Sun, 7 Jun

Your plan

  1. Pick up car at Treviso Airport · drive to Pisa
  2. Check in · park at hotel or outside ZTL
  3. Easy evening walk: Lungarno → Borgo Stretto → Piazza dei Cavalieri → Piazza dei Miracoli
  4. Simple dinner, early night · no tower climb

Activity picks

  • Lungarno at sunset — flat riverside walk, benches for rest stops.
  • Piazza dei Cavalieri — quieter than the tower area, plenty of café seating.
  • Piazza dei Miracoli exterior only — large flat lawn, easy benches, photo-friendly in evening light.
Elder note: if anyone is tired after the drive, skip Lungarno and Borgo Stretto — go directly to Piazza dei Miracoli for photos and dinner nearby. Total walking can be under 1 km.

Where to eat — close to Piazza dei Miracoli

Ristorante La BucaSit-down trattoria 2 min from the tower. Tuscan classics, parking nearby.
Osteria dei CavalieriCalm room, no stairs, great pasta. Reserve a ground-floor table.
Hotel restaurantAfter a long drive, eating at the hotel is the kindest choice. Many Pisa hotels offer half-board.
Gelateria De' ColtelliOutdoor benches on the river. Try if you take the Lungarno walk.
🍴 Try: cecìna, pasta with rabbit (coniglio), Bianco Pisano di San Torpè wine.

Day 2 · Pisa → Siena Class 7–10 pm

Mon, 8 Jun

Your plan

  1. 08:30–12:00 · Piazza dei Miracoli only (Tower exterior, Cathedral, Baptistery)
  2. Lunch in Pisa · collect car
  3. Drive directly to Siena · target arrival 15:30–16:30
  4. Check in, rest, light early dinner before class

Activity picks — Pisa morning

  • Cathedral (Duomo) — flat entry, beautiful interior, free with combo ticket.
  • Baptistery — go for the 30-min acoustic demonstrations (top of every half-hour). Sit on the bench.
  • Camposanto — only if energy allows; otherwise skip without regret.
  • Tower climb — skip. 251 narrow tilted steps with no central handrail, not elder-safe.
Elder note: the Opera della Primaziale ticket office offers free fast-track + step-free routing for the Cathedral and Baptistery on request.

Where to eat

Pisa lunch: Ristorante La BucaClosest sit-down to the tower, easy walk to car.
Pisa lunch alt: Trattoria S. OmobonoOld-Pisa cooking near Piazza delle Vettovaglie, ground-floor seating.
Siena dinner: Osteria Boccon del PreteSmall, quiet, reliable. 5-min walk from the Campo. Book 6:30 pm.
Siena takeaway: Pizzicheria de MiccoliIconic deli on Via di Città. Panini + pecorino for hotel-room dinner before class.
⏰ Class day: takeaway from Pizzicheria de Miccoli is the safest bet — at the hotel by 6 pm, no stress.

Day 3 · Slow Siena → Val d'Orcia Free evening

Tue, 9 Jun

Your plan

  1. Morning: Piazza del Campo, Siena Cathedral, slow coffee + lunch
  2. Afternoon scenic drive: Siena → Crete Senesi → San Quirico / Pienza
  3. 1–2 photo stops only · settle in for the evening

Activity picks — Siena

  • Piazza del Campo — the shell-shaped square is flat once you reach it. Sit on the brick slope, watch the world.
  • Siena Cathedral — book the OPA SI Pass and ask staff about step-free entry. The Piccolomini Library is the highlight; skip the Facciatone rooftop.
  • Torre del Mangia — skip (400 narrow steps).
  • Parking — Santa Caterina car park has covered escalators that lift you straight into the historic centre.

Activity picks — afternoon drive

  • Cappella della Madonna di Vitaleta viewpoint — safe pull-off, iconic chapel framed by cypresses. 5-min photo stop.
  • Bagno Vignoni preview — if energy allows, park and walk the flat central square.
  • Avoid unpaved cypress side-roads — rental cars and rough gravel don't mix.
Where to sleep: San Quirico d'Orcia is the easier base — flatter, more parking, restaurants closer to most hotels. Pienza is prettier but more uphill.

Where to eat

Siena lunch: Trattoria PapeiOn Piazza del Mercato just behind the Campo. Big shaded terrace, classic pici.
Siena sweet: NanniniStand-up bar for ricciarelli + espresso, or sit-down room upstairs (has elevator).
San Quirico dinner: Trattoria OsennaFamily-run, calm, pici al cinghiale. Ground floor.
Pienza dinner alt: La Buca delle FateStone cellar with even floor, very welcoming.
🍷 Try: pici, pappardelle al cinghiale, ricciarelli, Brunello or Rosso di Montalcino.

Day 4 · Val d'Orcia gentle loop Class 7–10 pm

Wed, 10 Jun

Your plan

  1. Pienza (1.5–2 h) — walk + view terrace + coffee
  2. Scenic drive: Pienza → Monticchiello road (30–45 min)
  3. San Quirico d'Orcia (1.5–2 h) — Horti Leonini + lunch
  4. Bagno Vignoni (45–60 min) — thermal-square walk
  5. Back to hotel by 17:00–17:30 · rest before class

Activity picks — Pienza

  • Corso il Rossellino — main street, mostly flat. Cheese shops, Renaissance façades.
  • Panoramic walk (Via dell'Amore) — short paved path with the best Val d'Orcia view. Benches.
  • Pecorino tasting — Marusco e Maria on the main street. Sit, taste, ship some home.

Activity picks — San Quirico + Bagno Vignoni

  • Horti Leonini — peaceful Renaissance garden, flat gravel, free, benches in shade.
  • Collegiata di San Quirico — small flat-entry church, 10 min.
  • Bagno Vignoni square — the village is one flat square around the thermal pool. The easiest stop of the day.
Elder note: all three villages are walkable in under 500 m end-to-end. Drop passengers near the square, park separately, and meet at a café.

Where to eat

Pienza coffee: Caffè La PostaOutdoor tables on the main street. Shaded seating.
San Quirico lunch: Trattoria al Vecchio FornoPretty garden, pici and pecorino specialities.
San Quirico alt: Il Conte MattoSlightly elevated terrace, valley view, ground-floor dining room.
Bagno Vignoni: Bar CentraleRight on the thermal square. Coffee + pastry with view.
🧀 Buy to bring home: Pecorino di Pienza, Val d'Orcia honey, saffron, a bottle of Rosso di Montalcino.

Day 5 · Val d'Orcia → Chianti wine lunch → Florence Class 7–10 pm

Thu, 11 Jun

Your plan

  1. 09:30 leave Val d'Orcia · drive ~1h 15min to Castellina in Chianti
  2. 11:30–13:30 Vineyard estate lunch in Chianti
  3. 13:30–15:30 drive to Florence (≈1 h)
  4. 15:30–16:00 arrive Florence · hotel outside ZTL
  5. Rest before class · simple dinner near hotel
Elder note: a sit-down vineyard lunch with a terrace view is much kinder on tired legs than another hill-town walk — and a more memorable goodbye to Tuscany.

🍷 Chianti vineyard lunch picks (book ahead)

  • Poggio Amorelli (Castellina in Chianti) — family-run, intimate tastings with light Tuscan lunch on the terrace. Easy parking. Ask for the "tasting + lunch" combo, English-speaking hosts.
  • Famiglia Mazzarrini — famigliamazzarrini.it. Sit-down tasting + cheese/cured meats on a shaded terrace, ~90 min. Family producer, very welcoming.
  • Castello di Albola (Radda in Chianti) — larger estate with step-free terrace, full lunch menu, panoramic Chianti view.
  • Rocca delle Macìe (Castellina) — professional setup, on-site restaurant + wine shop, accessible facilities.
📞 Booking script: "tasting + light lunch, party of 3, two seniors, please confirm step-free access and shaded terrace seating." Most Chianti estates are very accommodating.

Class-day driving math

  • Driver alert: Italy's limit is 0.5 g/L. At a tasting for 3, the driver should split pours or use the spit bucket. Police checks on the A1 are common.
  • Ask the winery for small pours (assaggio) for the driver and water + bread between wines.
  • For a stress-free version: a private driver pickup from Val d'Orcia → winery → Florence (Tuscan Driver, Florence Cab Tours) is ~€300 and removes all risk.

Where to eat

Lunch AT the wineryBest option — tasting + light lunch in one stop. No extra driving.
Backup: Trattoria La Torre (Castellina)Village trattoria, calm, ground floor, classic Chianti menu.
Florence dinner near hotelKeep it close. Ask your hotel for a quiet trattoria within 5 min walk.
🚗 Florence ZTL: Mon–Fri 07:30–20:00, Sat 07:30–16:00, plus summer night ZTL Thu/Fri/Sat. Park outside: Parcheggio Beccaria, Parterre, Sant'Ambrogio garages.

Day 6 · Florence relaxed main day Free evening

Fri, 12 Jun

Your plan

  1. Morning: Duomo exterior, Piazza Signoria, Palazzo Vecchio, Ponte Vecchio
  2. Sit-down lunch · break
  3. Afternoon: choose ONE indoor visit (Accademia / Uffizi / Palazzo Vecchio)
  4. Evening: optional taxi to Piazzale Michelangelo OR relaxed Oltrarno dinner

Activity picks (elder-friendly)

  • Accademia — much shorter than Uffizi (90 min vs 3 hours), step-free, benches in front of David. Best mobility-friendly pick.
  • Uffizi — has elevators and benches in every gallery. Book a timed entry online.
  • Duomo dome climb — skip (463 narrow spiral steps, no elevator).
  • Giotto's bell tower — also skip (414 steps).
  • Mercato Centrale (upstairs) — elevator, calm food-hall lunch with easy seating.
  • Piazzale Michelangelo — take a taxi up. The view is worth it; the staircase is not.
Elder note: Florence has a lot of cobblestones. Cushioned shoes, sit-down every 90 minutes, taxis without guilt.

Where to eat

Lunch: Trattoria MarioneCalm, central, no tourist rush, ground floor. Walk-in friendly.
Lunch hall: Mercato CentraleElevator to upstairs hall. Big shared tables, air-con.
Oltrarno dinner: Trattoria CammilloOld-school, white tablecloths, kind staff, no stairs. Reserve a week ahead.
Oltrarno alt: Il Santo BevitoreRefined Tuscan, calm room. Book ahead.
Gelato: Gelateria della PasseraTiny, Oltrarno, no queues.
🥩 Try: bistecca alla Fiorentina (shareable), pappa al pomodoro, ribollita, schiacciata bread.

Day 7 · Florence buffer day Rest day

Sat, 13 Jun

Three flexible options

  • Option A — Easy day: slow breakfast, Oltrarno / Santo Spirito walk, Bardini Gardens, café breaks, early dinner.
  • Option B — Missed sights: Uffizi / Accademia / Palazzo Pitti / Santa Croce. Pick one.
  • Option C — Practical day: laundry, repacking, confirm Bologna hotel parking, check route, early night.

Gentle activity picks

  • Bardini Gardens — smaller and flatter than Boboli, wisteria pergola + Florence panorama. Garden café.
  • Santa Croce — flat-entry basilica with the tombs of Michelangelo, Galileo, Machiavelli. Quiet inside.
  • Oltrarno artisan walk — Via Maggio + Borgo San Jacopo. Old workshops, flat, café stops.
  • Boboli — only if everyone is fresh; lots of hills and gravel.
Elder note: this is the day to not push. A good rest here makes Bologna and the Dolomites much more enjoyable.

Where to eat

Long lazy lunch: La GiostraTheatrical, kind staff, perfect for a long Saturday lunch.
Calm dinner: Trattoria 4 LeoniPiazza della Passera, outdoor seating, ground floor.
Light dinner: Antica Mescita San NiccolòCozy Tuscan, locals. Good for an early-night day.
Aperitivo: Le Volpi e l'UvaBehind Ponte Vecchio. Wine + cheese boards.

Day 8 · Florence → Bologna Easy

Sun, 14 Jun

Your plan

  1. Slow breakfast · check out
  2. Drive to Bologna (~1.5 h on the A1)
  3. Park at hotel or pre-arranged garage
  4. Compact afternoon walk + early dinner

Activity picks — Bologna (gentle)

  • Piazza Maggiore + Basilica di San Petronio — flat-entry basilica, benches all around.
  • Portico walks — Bologna's 40 km of UNESCO covered porticoes mean shaded, weather-proof, smooth walking. Perfect for elders.
  • Quadrilatero food market — small flat alleys with delis, fishmongers, salumerie. Browse, taste, buy.
  • Santo Stefano (Seven Churches) — flat entry, peaceful interior, easy 30-min visit.
  • Two Towers (Asinelli) — skip the climb (498 wooden steps); enjoy from the square.
  • Archiginnasio — flat-entry old library with the anatomical theatre. Calm stop.
Elder note: Bologna's porticoes are the city's superpower for slow travel — shade in summer, no rain worries, flat walking. Build the day around them.

Where to eat

Lunch arrival: Trattoria Anna MariaHandmade tortellini and tagliatelle al ragù. Calm, ground floor.
Dinner: Osteria dell'OrsaCasual, locals, big portions. Walk-in friendly.
Refined: Trattoria di Via SerraSlightly outside the centre — take a taxi. Some of the best ragù in Bologna.
Aperitivo: Osteria del SoleHistoric wine-only osteria (since 1465). BYO food. Atmospheric.
🍝 Try: tortellini in brodo, tagliatelle al ragù, mortadella, crescentine fritte, Lambrusco.

Day 9 · Bologna → Val Gardena Class 7–10 pm

Mon, 15 Jun

Your plan

  1. Breakfast in Bologna · optional short walk if fresh
  2. Drive Bologna → Bolzano → Val Gardena (~4 h with breaks)
  3. Motorway service / coffee stops only
  4. Target arrival: 16:00–16:30 in Ortisei / Santa Cristina / Selva
  5. Check in · simple dinner or snacks · class

Where to sleep — base picks

  • Ortisei (recommended) — main hub, best for lifts, restaurants, easy strolls in the village. Best for elders.
  • Santa Cristina — quieter middle of the valley, very central for both Seceda and Alpe di Siusi.
  • Selva — closest to the mountain passes, more rustic feel.
Alpine note: the Val Gardena road from Bolzano (SS242) has switchbacks. Build in extra time and a coffee stop in Castelrotto or Ponte Gardena to break it up. Avoid arriving after dark.

Where to eat

Roadside coffee: Autogrill Reno EstBest motorway stop between Bologna and Bolzano.
Ortisei dinner: TubladelCozy Ladin-Tyrolean cuisine, low ceilings + cushioned benches.
Class-day takeaway: Bäckerei VinatzerBakery + deli on the main street. Schlutzkrapfen + apfelstrudel for hotel-room dinner.
Hotel half-boardMost Val Gardena hotels offer half-board — on a class day, it's the easiest choice.

Day 10 · Seceda by lift Mountain day

Tue, 16 Jun

Your plan

  1. Morning: Ortisei → Furnes gondola → Furnes → Seceda cable car
  2. Enjoy the main Seceda viewpoint at the top
  3. Mountain hut lunch / coffee
  4. Short flat walk near the top only if comfortable
  5. Return to Ortisei mid-afternoon, rest before dinner

Activity picks

  • Seceda viewpoint — 5-min walk from the top cable car station to one of the most iconic Dolomites views (the famous jagged ridge).
  • Sun terrace at the cable car station — sit, look, photograph. Benches and full panorama, no hike required.
  • Short walk towards Baita Sofie — 25 min mostly flat path on the meadow, with rifugio for refreshments.
  • Skip the Fermeda chairlift — doesn't open until later in June anyway, and the path is steeper.
Alpine note: the top of Seceda is at 2,500 m — bring a warm layer even in June, and take it slow for the first 30 min to adjust to altitude. The lift does the work; you just need to walk gently.

Booking + weather

  • Book Seceda lift tickets online the night before (valgardena-groeden.com). Cheaper round-trip than two singles.
  • Check webcam at sunrise — if the top is in cloud, swap with Day 11 plan (Alpe di Siusi).
  • The Ortisei–Furnes gondola usually starts ~8:30; aim for 9:00–9:30 to avoid the rush.

Where to eat

On the mountain: Baita SofieSun terrace overlooking the ridge. Knödel, polenta, strudel.
Mountain hut: Rifugio Firenze (Regensburger Hütte)Slightly farther but quieter, classic alpine fare.
Ortisei dinner: Anna StubenRefined Ladin cuisine in Hotel Gardena Grödnerhof. Book.
Ortisei casual: Pizzeria Mauriz KellerCellar pizzeria, low light, comfortable seating.
🥟 Try: canederli (knödel) in brodo, schlutzkrapfen (spinach-ricotta ravioli), tirtlan, kaiserschmarrn, apfelstrudel.

Day 11 · Alpe di Siusi OR Val di Funes Class 7–10 pm

Wed, 17 Jun

Option A — Alpe di Siusi by gondola (recommended)

  1. Morning: Ortisei → Alpe di Siusi gondola (Mont Sëuc)
  2. Short flat walk on Europe's largest alpine meadow
  3. Mountain hut lunch or coffee stop
  4. Return to Ortisei by early afternoon
  5. Rest at hotel before class
Why this works: Alpe di Siusi is famously flat (huge meadow at 2,000 m). No hike required — the view is right at the lift station, with benches and panoramic restaurants.

Option B — Val di Funes scenic drive

  1. Drive Val Gardena → Val di Funes (~45 min)
  2. Santa Maddalena viewpoint — the postcard church with Odle peaks behind
  3. San Giovanni in Ranui chapel — second iconic photo stop
  4. Coffee in Funes village
  5. Return to Val Gardena

Recommended choice

  • Choose A (Alpe di Siusi) if the weather is clear and everyone enjoyed Seceda — the meadow is gentler than Seceda and very photogenic.
  • Choose B (Val di Funes) if anyone is tired of lifts, or if rain/clouds make a drive better than a mountain top.

Return time

  • Return to hotel by 17:00–17:30.
  • Light dinner or hotel half-board · class 7–10 pm.

Where to eat

Alpe di Siusi: Gostner SchwaigeFamous mountain hut with herb-garden cuisine. Reserve.
Alpe di Siusi: Sanon HütteHearty knödel + view. Closer to the gondola top.
Val di Funes: Gasthof Pension GeislerFamily-run in Santa Maddalena. Tyrolean classics.
Ortisei takeaway: Bäckerei VinatzerSame as Monday — perfect for class-day room dinner.

Day 12 · Val Gardena → Dobbiaco Class 7–10 pm

Thu, 18 Jun

Your plan

  1. Slow breakfast · check out
  2. Drive Val Gardena → Passo Gardena → Val Badia → Pustertal → Dobbiaco (~2.5 h with stops)
  3. Optional stop: Lago di Dobbiaco lakeside walk + coffee
  4. Target arrival: 16:00–16:30
  5. Check in · rest · class

Activity picks — scenic drive

  • Passo Gardena — one of the most beautiful mountain passes, safe pull-offs for photos. Driving is slow but rewarding.
  • Corvara / La Villa — quick coffee stop in Val Badia if you need a break.
  • Lake Dobbiaco — easy flat lakeside path, beautiful pine forest, great low-effort arrival stop.
  • Skip any big detours — keep it a class-day transfer.

Where to sleep — base picks

  • Dobbiaco (Toblach) — practical, flat town, central for Tre Cime + Braies + Misurina.
  • San Candido (Innichen) — slightly more charming pedestrian centre, great cafés. Equally good base.
  • Both are 6 km apart on a flat valley road.
Alpine note: the Passo Gardena road has tight bends. Two pull-offs near the top are safe for photos; otherwise keep moving. Carry water — altitude + sun is dehydrating.

Where to eat

Lake Dobbiaco: GratschwirtHistoric inn near the lake, garden seating, easy menu.
Dobbiaco dinner: Tilia (Hotel Cristallo)Refined Tyrolean, beautiful dining room. Book.
San Candido dinner: Mountain Spa RestaurantAt Leitlhof Hotel, ground floor, locals + visitors.
Class-day takeaway: Bäckerei Hofmann (San Candido)Bakery, salumi, hot strudel.

Day 13 · Tre Cime / Lake Misurina Mountain day

Fri, 19 Jun

Your plan

  1. Drive Dobbiaco → Lake Misurina (~30 min)
  2. Continue up the Tre Cime toll road to Rifugio Auronzo (if booked)
  3. Short viewpoint walk only — do not attempt the full 10 km loop
  4. Coffee / lunch at Rifugio Auronzo
  5. Return via Lake Misurina · lakeside coffee + photo
  6. Back to hotel for rest

Activity picks

  • Rifugio Auronzo terrace (2,320 m) — sit on the deck with three peaks of Tre Cime right in front of you. No walk needed.
  • Short flat walk towards Rifugio Lavaredo — only the first 10 min are mostly flat. Turn back before any climb.
  • Lake Misurina — flat 2 km lakeside path with benches, or just sit at a lakeside café.
  • Avoid the full Tre Cime loop (10 km, exposed, no shelter from sun or weather).
Booking critical: the Tre Cime toll road (SR48bis from Misurina) charges ~€30 per car and parking at Rifugio Auronzo fills early. Reserve a slot at 3cime.tre-cime.it as soon as June dates open. Arrive before 10 am or after 3 pm.

Where to eat

Top: Rifugio AuronzoSelf-service terrace + table-service room. Big portions, fair prices. Views are the main course.
Top alt: Rifugio Lavaredo20-min walk from Auronzo (only attempt if comfortable). Quieter, family-run.
Misurina: Chalet Lago AntornoSlightly above the lake, panoramic terrace, great trout and goulash.
Dobbiaco dinner: Tilia or GratschwirtRepeat from Thursday — both are great, low-effort options.

Day 14 · Lago di Braies OR Cortina Last full day

Sat, 20 Jun

Option A — Lago di Braies (recommended if booking works)

  1. Drive Dobbiaco → Braies (~30 min)
  2. Park (pre-booked) and walk the easier eastern lakeside section
  3. Coffee on the lake terrace
  4. Optional short rowing-boat ride if weather is calm
  5. Back to hotel by lunch or early afternoon
Booking critical: Lago di Braies has strict access rules in June. Either pre-book a parking slot at prags.bz, or take the shuttle bus from Dobbiaco station. Without a booking, cars are turned away. Arrive early (8–9 am) for the best light and quiet.

Option B — Cortina d'Ampezzo easy day

  1. Drive Dobbiaco → Cortina (~30 min)
  2. Walk Corso Italia (pedestrian, mostly flat)
  3. Long lunch on a terrace
  4. Optional scenic drive to Passo Falzarego (1 h round trip) only if energy allows
  5. Return to Dobbiaco

Recommended choice

  • Choose A (Braies) if the parking booking went through — it's the most photogenic Dolomite lake and an easy lakeside visit.
  • Choose B (Cortina) if anyone is tired of nature stops, if Braies booking failed, or if the weather is poor.

Where to eat

Braies lakeside: Hotel Pragser Wildsee restaurantHistoric 1899 hotel right on the lake. Terrace. Reserve.
Cortina lunch: Tivoli (Michelin)Refined alpine cuisine just above town. Reserve well ahead.
Cortina casual: Al CaminMountain-style trattoria, locals, ground floor, easy menu.
Farewell dinner: Tilia (Dobbiaco)Save your best dinner reservation for tonight. Memorable end to the trip.

Day 15 · Departure Travel day

Sun, 21 Jun

Your plan

  1. Breakfast · check out
  2. Drive to airport with generous buffer for mountain roads + Sunday traffic
  3. Return rental car at airport

Drive time notes

  • Dobbiaco → Treviso Airport: ~2 h 45 min via A27. Add 1 h buffer for weekend traffic + fuel + car return.
  • Dobbiaco → Venice Marco Polo: ~3 h via A27. Similar buffer.
  • Dobbiaco → Innsbruck: ~2 h via the Brenner Pass — easiest option if flying from Austria.
If your flight is early: consider moving closer to the airport on Saturday 20 June night (e.g., Treviso or Venice mainland hotel) instead of sleeping in Dobbiaco. Cuts stress to almost zero.

To book in advance

Tuscany

  • Pisa Cathedral / Baptistery combo ticket (skip Tower climb)
  • Siena OPA SI Pass — ask about step-free entry
  • Florence: one of Uffizi or Accademia — Accademia is the elder-friendlier pick
  • Chianti vineyard lunch near Castellina (Poggio Amorelli, Famiglia Mazzarrini, Castello di Albola, Rocca delle Macìe)
  • Florence Friday or Saturday dinner reservations
  • Hotels with reliable parking outside ZTL in each city

Bologna

  • Hotel parking or garage access — confirm ZTL permit process
  • Sunday dinner reservation (Anna Maria or Trattoria di Via Serra)

Dolomites — critical bookings

  • Seceda lift tickets — book online at valgardena-groeden.com the night before
  • Alpe di Siusi gondola — same, plus check parking restrictions if driving (Castelrotto road is limited 9 am – 5 pm)
  • Tre Cime toll road + Rifugio Auronzo parking — reserve at 3cime.tre-cime.it as soon as June slots open
  • Lago di Braies parking — book at prags.bz or plan to use the shuttle from Dobbiaco
  • Check lift opening dates close to travel — early-June operations vary by lift and weather
  • Farewell dinner reservation (Tilia in Dobbiaco or similar)

Class-day rule

  • Hotel by 17:00–17:30 on Mon/Wed/Thu
  • Light dinner or takeaway — never a sit-down restaurant booking on a class night
  • Check Wi-Fi as soon as you check in
  • No late transfers, no driving after class
